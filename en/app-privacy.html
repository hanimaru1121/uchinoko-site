Privacy Policy (Uchinoko Live — Custom Pet Data and Accounts)

This policy explains how Takuya Ito ("we", "us"), a sole proprietor based in New York, United States, handles personal information in the Uchinoko Live custom "Pet Data" service and the accounts used to receive Pet Datas (together, the "Service").

Short version: we collect only what we need to make and deliver your Pet Data and to take payment. Before any photo goes to an AI service, we crop it to show only your pet and strip location data, so your name, email and face never go to the AI services. We do not sell your information, show ads, or use analytics trackers.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal information is Takuya Ito, New York, United States. Contact: hello@uchinoko.live.

2. What we collect

Information When and from where
Your name (a nickname is fine), email address, and our messages When you contact us by email
Pet photos and the data embedded in them (date, time, location), plus any people or background that appear When you send photos to ask about an order

What happens inside the app (petting times, mood, the pet's current pose, etc.) and any photo you choose as a background stay on your device (in the browser version, in that browser) and are not sent to us. We do not use advertising or usage-analytics tools.

6. Deleting your account and asking us to delete information

  • You can delete your account in Uchinoko Live at any time. When you do:
  • photos stored on our server are deleted;
  • Pet Data delivered only to you is withdrawn and deleted (it is also removed from your devices and cannot be received a second time);
  • your Sign in with Apple authorisation is revoked;
  • your contact details are removed from our order records. Payment records (payment ID, amount, date) are kept for the period above for accounting and tax.
  • To delete information held outside the app (emails, our working folders, etc.) or to withdraw your example permission, email us. We will act promptly and tell you when it is done.

7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, give you a copy, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Email hello@uchinoko.live. We will confirm it is you, and reply free of charge, normally within 14 days (and always within the time limit the law gives).

If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, you also have the right to restrict processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing before withdrawal). You can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). We would appreciate the chance to sort out your concern first.

If you are in California or another US state with a privacy law: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information. Our iPhone, Mac and browser versions contain no advertising or analytics tools and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so we do not respond differently to "Do Not Track" signals.

8. Security

  • Photos, Pet Data and order records are kept in private, access-controlled storage that only we can access. Pet Data is delivered only to the account registered as their owner, through time-limited links.
  • Our work computer and accounts are protected with sign-in security such as two-step verification.
  • If a breach of your information occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. Children

The Service is for adults (18 or over). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.
