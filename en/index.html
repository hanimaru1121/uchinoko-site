From your photos to a little pet that hangs out on your screen

Uchinoko Live is a made-to-order service. From the photos you send, we create your "pet data": a set of photo-style portraits and short video clips of your pet. Load it into the Uchinoko Live app, and your pet stands, sits, lies down, and sleeps in a corner of your screen while you go about your day. Uchi no ko is how people in Japan often refer to their pet — literally, "the kid at our house."

Everyday life

Pet it, and it responds the way your pet does

Tap pet, treat, or water, and your pet does just that. The reactions aren't the same every time. The maker's dog usually melts when she's stroked — but now and then she ducks away when you go for the top of her head. Tell us your pet's habits, and we'll work them into the clips.

At night it mostly sleeps. Leave it alone for a while and it starts asking for attention or gets a little hungry. Skip a day of care and nothing bad happens. It doesn't talk, and it doesn't grin like a person.

How it works

From first email to delivery

  1. Send photos

    Email us 3–5 photos of your pet. A full-body shot, a close-up of the face, and a side view make it much easier. The review is free.

  2. We tell you if we can make it

    We look at your photos and decide whether we can make a convincing likeness — usually within 24 hours. If we can, we email you a summary and a payment link. If not, we tell you before you pay.

  3. Pay

    Pay up front by card on the payment page. Then sign in to Uchinoko Live with the Apple Account you’ll use to view your pet, and send us the email address shown next to “ログイン中” (signed in). If you chose Hide My Email, it will be an …@privaterelay.appleid.com address — that’s fine.

  4. We make it

    We email you when production starts, and refine everything side by side with your photos until it looks like your pet.

  5. Delivery

    Within 14 days of receiving both your payment and your photos, we deliver your pet's data to your account. Open the iPhone or Mac app, and your pet is there.

  6. Redos

    If anything doesn't feel like your pet, let us know within 14 days of delivery. We redo it for free, up to two times.

Questions

Questions

Is this made with AI?

Yes, AI is part of how we make it. We never hand over raw output — every result is checked against your photos until it looks like your pet. The services that process your photos are listed in our Privacy Policy.

What kind of photos should I send?

3–5 clear photos with only your pet in them. A full-body shot, a close-up of the face, and a side view work best. Good light and visible fur texture help. We'll look at them and let you know whether we can make your pet.

Can you make pets other than dogs and cats?

Yes — budgies and hamsters too (see the samples on this page). Send us photos and we'll tell you whether we can make it.

I can't take new photos right now. Is that okay?

Yes. We can work from photos you already have. If new photos are hard to get, just send what you have and we'll take a look.

Which devices can I use? Does it work on Android?

You can view your pet on iPhone (iOS 17 or later, US App Store only) and on Mac (macOS 14 or later). You’ll need an Apple Account to sign in. It also opens in Google Chrome on a computer, but the iPhone and Mac apps are where your pet lives on your Home Screen and desktop. The app screens are currently in Japanese. There’s no Android app. If you don’t have an iPhone with a US App Store account or a Mac, please ask us before you order.

What if it doesn't look like my pet?

Tell us within 14 days of delivery and we'll redo it for free, up to two times. If it still doesn't feel like your pet, ask within 30 days of delivery and we'll refund you in full. You decide whether it looks right.

How long does it take?

Within 14 days of receiving both your payment and your photos. If we expect a delay, we'll tell you before the deadline. If we can't deliver within 30 days, you can cancel for a full refund.

How do I pay, and are there other costs?

After we confirm we can make your pet, we send a secure payment page (Stripe). You pay once, up front, by card — Apple Pay or Google Pay may also appear. $199 is the total; any sales tax or VAT is included. Stripe may show the amount in your local currency, and your card issuer may add its own foreign-transaction fee. You can cancel for a full refund at any time before delivery. If we ever close the service, we'll give at least 90 days' notice and, on request, send you your pet's videos as files.

What happens to my photos?

We use them only to make your pet. To make your pet, we may send them to outside services (in the US and elsewhere), listed in our Privacy Policy. Before sending, we crop each photo to just your pet and remove location data. We delete your photos 90 days after delivery (about 7 days if you don't go ahead). We only show your pet as an example if you say yes when we ask after delivery. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does it talk or smile like a person?

No. It only does things your pet would do — grooming, yawning, stretching, sighing. Tell us about your pet's habits and we'll include as many as we can.
