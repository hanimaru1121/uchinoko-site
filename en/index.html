From your photos to a little pet that hangs out on your screen

Uchinoko Live is a made-to-order service. From the photos you send, we create your "pet data": a set of photo-style portraits and short video clips of your pet. Load it into the Uchinoko Live app, and your pet stands, sits, lies down, and sleeps in a corner of your screen while you go about your day. Uchi no ko is how people in Japan often refer to their pet — literally, "the kid at our house."

What you get

Add a move your pet is known for

A jump, a roll-over, a favorite trick — add any move your pet often does for US$15 each. Your pet shows it off now and then on its own. If a move is too hard to make, we'll tell you before you pay.

Everyday life

Pet it, and it responds the way your pet does

Tap pet, treat, or water, and your pet does just that. The reactions aren't the same every time. Our sample dog usually melts when she's stroked — but now and then she ducks away when you go for the top of her head. Tell us your pet's habits, and we'll work them into the clips.

At night it mostly sleeps. Leave it alone for a while and it starts asking for attention or gets a little hungry. Skip a day of care and nothing bad happens. It doesn't talk, and it doesn't grin like a person.

Mac desktop

How it works

From first email to delivery

  1. Send photos

    Use the form to send three photos of your pet: full body, face close-up, and side view. The review is free.

  2. We tell you if we can make it

    We look at your photos and decide whether we can make a convincing likeness, usually within 24 hours, including any move you'd like added. If we can, we email you a summary and a payment link. If not, we tell you before you pay.

  3. Pay

    Pay up front by card on the payment page. Then sign in to Uchinoko Live with the Apple Account you’ll use to view your pet, and send us the email address shown as signed in. If you chose Hide My Email, it will be an …@privaterelay.appleid.com address — that’s fine.

  4. Approve the portraits first

    Within 7 days of receiving your payment and photos, we send portraits of your pet in its poses. If they don't look right, we redraw them up to two times. If it still doesn't feel like your pet, we refund you in full.

  5. We make the videos and deliver

    Within 14 days of your approval, we deliver the full set of clips, plus pixel art for your widget, to your account. Open the iPhone or Mac app, and your pet is there.

  6. Redos on the moves

    If any move doesn't feel like your pet, tell us within 14 days of delivery. We redo it for free — up to two rounds and 10 clips in total.

Questions

Questions

Is this made with AI?

Yes, AI is part of how we make it. We never hand over raw output — every result is checked against your photos until it looks like your pet. The services that process your photos are listed in our Privacy Policy.

What kind of photos should I send?

3–5 clear photos with only your pet in them. A full-body shot, a close-up of the face, and a side view work best. Good light and visible fur texture help. We'll look at them and let you know whether we can make your pet.

Can you make pets other than dogs and cats?

Yes — budgies and hamsters too (see the samples on this page). Send us photos and we'll tell you whether we can make it.

I can't take new photos right now. Is that okay?

Yes. We can work from photos you already have. If new photos are hard to get, just send what you have and we'll take a look.

Can you work from old or low-quality photos?

We'll look and tell you. Older phone photos and slightly soft shots can work if your pet's fur and face are readable. Photos that are very dark (strong backlight) or where your pet is tiny and far away are harder, and we'll say so in our reply. We decide before you pay, so there's no cost if we can't make it.

I only have one or two photos.

Three photos (full body, face close-up, side view) make it easiest, but send what you have. If an angle is missing, we'll tell you which extra photos would help after we've looked.

Can you add a move my pet is known for?

Yes. You can add a move like "jump" or "roll over" for US$15 each — just include it when you order. There's no button for it; your pet does it on its own from time to time, and you can't choose when. If we don't think we can make a move, we tell you before you pay. If we try and can't make it, we refund that move.

Does it come with the pixel-art widget?

Yes, at no extra cost. Pixel art is drawn with small dots, so it can't show fine fur or small markings; we capture your pet's overall look — colours, markings, ear shape. Redraws come out of the same move-redo allowance.

Which devices can I use? Does it work on Android?

You can view your pet on iPhone (iOS 17 or later, US App Store only) and on Mac (macOS 14 or later). You’ll need an Apple Account to sign in. The apps are in English. There’s no Android or Windows app. If you don’t have an iPhone with a US App Store account or a Mac, please ask us before you order.

What if it doesn't look like my pet?

Before we make any videos, you check portraits of your pet. If they don't look right, we redraw them up to two times, and if it still doesn't feel like your pet, we refund you in full. You decide whether it looks right. Once you approve the portraits, we don't offer refunds on the grounds that the clips don't feel like your pet. We redo moves that don't feel like your pet for free within 14 days of delivery (up to two rounds and 10 clips in total). If your pet looks clearly different from the approved portraits in a clip, we fix it without counting it as a redo. Your rights under consumer law are unaffected.

Can I cancel?

Until you approve the portraits, you can cancel at any time for a full refund. If you cancel after approving but before delivery, we refund the price minus US$35 for the cost of making the clips. After delivery, there is no refund for cancelling. This doesn't affect your rights under consumer law if something is faulty.

How long does it take?

Within 7 days of receiving your payment and photos, we send the portraits. Within 14 days of your approval, we deliver the full set of clips. If we expect a delay, we'll tell you before the deadline. If we can't deliver within 30 days (not counting the time we're waiting for your reply on the portraits), you can cancel for a full refund.

How do I pay, and are there other costs?

After we confirm we can make your pet, we send a secure payment page (Stripe). You pay once, up front, by card — Apple Pay or Google Pay may also appear. $199 is the total (plus US$15 for each extra move you choose); any sales tax or VAT is included. Stripe may show the amount in your local currency, and your card issuer may add its own foreign-transaction fee. If we ever close the service, we'll give at least 90 days' notice and, on request, send you your pet's videos as files.

What happens to my photos?

We use them only to make your pet. To make your pet, we may send them to outside services (in the US and elsewhere), listed in our Privacy Policy. Before sending, we crop each photo to just your pet and remove location data. We delete your photos 90 days after delivery (about 7 days if you don't go ahead). We only show your pet as an example if you say yes when we ask after delivery. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does it talk or smile like a person?

No. It only does things your pet would do — grooming, yawning, stretching, sighing. Tell us about your pet's habits and we'll include as many as we can.
