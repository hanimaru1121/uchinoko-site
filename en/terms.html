1. What we make

  1. From photos of your pet that you send us, we create a set of photo-style artwork and about 20 short video clips (the "Pet Data") and deliver it to your account.
  2. You view the Pet Data in the Uchinoko Live apps for iPhone and Mac, and you can also open it in Google Chrome on a computer. The iPhone app is available on the US App Store only (not in Canada, Australia or New Zealand). The app screens are currently in Japanese. Whether the iPhone app stays on the App Store is up to Apple; if it stops being available, we will keep your Pet Data viewable on Mac and in the browser, and on request we will give you the videos of your Pet Data as files you can save on your own device (the same use limits in Section 10 apply). Except in that case and as described in Section 15, we do not deliver the Pet Data as separate files.
  3. The artwork and videos are created with AI tools (Section 5) and then checked and finished by a person. They are not hand-drawn pictures or filmed footage.
  4. The Pet Data includes settings that let your pet react inside the app (for example, to petting or treats). The reaction system is the same for every pet; we do not build new app features for an individual order.
  5. If you like, you can add moves you'd like your pet to do (for example, "jump" or "roll over") for an extra fee per move ("requested moves"). A requested move is added as a clip that your pet does on its own from time to time. There is no button to call it up, and you can't choose when it appears. It is not a new app feature. If we don't think we can make a move, we tell you before you pay.
  6. The Pet Data includes a pixel-art version of your pet for the Home Screen widget, at no extra cost. Because pixel art is drawn with small dots, it can't show fine fur texture or small markings; we capture your pet's overall look, such as colours, markings and ear shape.
